FORMULA FOR RELATIVE PATHS:
  Distance = (Target Level - Current Level) × "../"
  
EXAMPLE 1 - From src/pages/home/ to src/assets/css/:
  • Current: 2 levels (pages/home)
  • Target: 1 level (assets/css)
  • Distance: 1 level up (../../)
  • Result: ../../assets/css/

EXAMPLE 2 - From src/pages/gestion/asignacion-voluntarios/ to src/login/
  • Current: 4 levels (pages/gestion/asignacion-voluntarios)
  • Target: 2 levels (pages/login)
  • Distance: 2 levels up + lateral
  • Result: ../../login/

EXAMPLE 3 - From src/pages/gestion/tiendas/ to src/components/header/
  • Current: 4 levels (pages/gestion/tiendas)
